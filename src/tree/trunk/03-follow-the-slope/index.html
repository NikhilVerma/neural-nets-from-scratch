Follow the slope

What engineers call this
w and b
The two knobs, in every codebase you will ever read: w for weight (our multiplier) and b for bias (our add-on).
derivative
Our slope: how far one number moves when another moves by a hair.
gradient
All the slopes at once, one per knob, collected into a list.
gradient descent
The whole method on this page: work out the slopes, step against them, repeat.
learning rate
Our step size. The most fiddled-with number in the field.
epoch
One pass through the whole set of examples — one press of the button.
analytic vs numerical gradients
Reading the slope off the formula (this lesson) versus measuring it by poking (last lesson). Same answer; one of them is affordable.
underfitting, model capacity
What the curve demo shows: the machine is wrong not because it trained badly but because it cannot represent the answer.
