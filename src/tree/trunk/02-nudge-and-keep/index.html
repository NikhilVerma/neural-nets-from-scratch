Nudge and keep

What engineers call this
hill climbing
The strategy on this page: from where you stand, look only at your immediate neighbours and move to the best one. Downhill here, because low scores win.
coordinate descent
The detail that we move one knob at a time instead of all of them together.
finite differences
Working out which way a formula is heading by evaluating it at two nearby points and comparing — our up-nudge and our down-nudge, exactly.
step size, step decay
Our nudge size, and the habit of halving it when progress stops.
function evaluations
Our test-runs. This is the number people count when they compare methods.
